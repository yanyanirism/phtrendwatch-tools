BMI Calculator

Calculate adult BMI in metric or imperial units.

What is BMI?

Body Mass Index is weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared. It's a quick screening number used for adults, not a diagnosis.

Adult BMI categories (WHO)

BMICategory
Below 18.5Underweight
18.5 – 24.9Healthy weight
25 – 29.9Overweight
30 and aboveObesity

Limits of BMI

BMI doesn't distinguish muscle from fat, and healthy ranges differ for children, during pregnancy, for older adults and for some ethnic groups. Talk to a healthcare professional about what's right for you.

This calculator is for general information only and is not medical advice.
