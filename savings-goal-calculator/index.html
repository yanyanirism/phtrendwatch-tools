Savings Goal Calculator

Tell us your goal and deadline and we'll work out the monthly amount to set aside.

How to reach a savings goal

Break a big number into a monthly habit. Subtract what you've already saved from the goal, then divide by the number of months you have. If your savings earn interest, you need a little less each month — this calculator accounts for that.

Tips that make it stick

  • Automate it. Set up a transfer for the day after payday so the money moves before you can spend it.
  • Use separate pots. Keeping each goal in its own account or "pot" (often called sinking funds) makes progress visible.
  • Track progress. Seeing a goal fill up is the best motivation to keep going.

Frequently asked questions

Should I build an emergency fund first?

Many people aim for three to six months of essential expenses before saving for other goals, so a surprise bill doesn't end up on a credit card.
