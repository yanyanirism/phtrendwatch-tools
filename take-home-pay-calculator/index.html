Take-Home Pay Calculator

See your net monthly pay after SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG and income tax.

How take-home pay is computed

Your net pay is your basic salary minus your share of government contributions and the income tax withheld by your employer:

take-home pay = salary − SSS − PhilHealth − Pag-IBIG − withholding tax

The deductions (2025 rates)

DeductionEmployee share
SSS5% of your monthly salary credit (₱5,000 to ₱35,000), so ₱250 to ₱1,750
PhilHealth2.5% of your basic salary (floor ₱10,000, ceiling ₱100,000), so ₱250 to ₱2,500
Pag-IBIG2% of up to ₱10,000, so at most ₱200
Withholding taxBased on the TRAIN Law table, after the contributions above are subtracted

Earning ₱20,833 a month or less in taxable income means no income tax, since that is ₱250,000 a year.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my payslip slightly different?

Employers may compute tax per pay period, include overtime or allowances in taxable pay, or apply year-end adjustments. Minimum wage earners are exempt from income tax on their minimum wage.

Is my 13th month pay taxed?

Not up to ₱90,000 combined with other benefits. Use our 13th month pay calculator to check.
